Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Undead Ends is a single-player puzzle game. It works fully offline; online features (friends, the friends' daily results, accounts, cloud save) are optional. We collect as little as we can and never sell personal data.

Undead Ends was previously called DETOUR.

Ads

Undead Ends shows ads through Google AdMob (rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional ads between levels; never with Remove Ads). In the EEA/UK you are asked for consent first (Google's consent form). On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier; you get the same number of ads either way. Google's use of data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can change your choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where available).

Children

Undead Ends is rated for ages 12+ (cartoon zombies, no gore). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

Delete your online account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account: your profile, friends, daily results, ratings, feedback answers, cloud save and credentials are deleted immediately. For any other request (access, correction) see Support.
