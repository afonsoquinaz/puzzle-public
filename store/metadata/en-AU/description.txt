Zombies are shuffling towards the survivors' house. You get a few barricades.

Drop them on the roads so every zombie's route leads it into a pit before it reaches the door. Plan first, press GO, and watch your plan play out. Pure logic: no reflexes, no timers, no luck.

SEE EVERY ROUTE
Each zombie's path is drawn on the board and redraws the instant you place a barricade, so you always know where the horde will walk.

144 LEVELS, TWELVE CHAPTERS
Twelve chapters across DEADGRID landmarks, from the Garden to Stonehenge, rated Easy to Nightmare. Every level is proven solvable and has a known par. Doing well? Perfect clears let you skip ahead.

NEW TWISTS AS YOU GO
Deep pits that swallow two, tanks that smash straight through your barricades, runners that race them, pressure plates that swing gates open, one-way roads, sewer tunnels and cracked road that caves in.

BEAT PAR

STEP AND REPLAY
