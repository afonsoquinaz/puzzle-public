Open an issue at github.com/afonsoquinaz/puzzle-public/issues. Include your device, iOS version and, if it is about your account, your DETOUR username (Settings → Account). Never post your password or recovery code.

Common questions

How do the zombies choose their road? Every move, each zombie steps to the neighbouring road tile that is closest to the house, going around barricades (tanks ignore barricades). The lines on the board show each route; a solid line ends at the pit it will fall into.

Why was my barricade refused? Every zombie (except tanks) must always have a way to the house. A barricade that would trap one is refused.

What is par? The fewest barricades that can win. Win with par for three stars.

I got a new phone. On the old phone: Settings → Account → Protect my account (set a password, write down the recovery code). On the new phone: Settings → Account → Sign in.

I bought Remove Ads. Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete account (tap twice). This deletes your online account (name, friends, daily results, cloud save). Progress stored on the phone stays until you delete the app.
